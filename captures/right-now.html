fortunes that are true right now

a paper frog, for Bella, who made the first one

fold
  1. Cut out the square.
  2. Turn it over so the frog is face down.
  3. Fold all four corners into the middle.
  4. Turn it over. Fold the four corners into the middle again.
  5. Fold it in half both ways, and open it back up.
  6. Turn it frog-side up. Slide your thumbs and pointer fingers under the four frog flaps and push the points together.
play
  1. Ask the frog anything.
  2. Open and close it once for each letter of your name.
  3. Pick a number you can see. Open and close it that many times.
  4. Pick a number. Lift its flap. Read what is true right now.
